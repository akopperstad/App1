### Description
```text
Pulse is the workout timer built for the gym floor: giant digits you can read from across the room, cues you can hear over the music, and timing that never drifts.

Pick a format, set it up in seconds and train. Pulse counts, beeps, speaks and buzzes, so your eyes can stay on the bar.

EVERY FORMAT YOU TRAIN
• AMRAP: one block or several with rest between. Tap to count rounds and see every split.
• EMOM, E2MOM, E90s: any interval length, with rotating movements and rest minutes.
• For Time: count up with an optional time cap and target rounds. Finishes automatically on your last round.
• Tabata: the classic 20/10 × 8, or your own version, across multiple sets.
• Intervals: any work/rest, rounds, sets and rest between sets. HIIT, running repeats, circuits, boxing rounds.
• Death By: the rep target climbs every minute until you can't keep up. Pulse tracks how many rounds you survived.
• Custom: build any sequence of timed steps and untimed "tap when done" steps (run 400 m, then rest 90 s) and repeat it for as many rounds as you like.
• Countdown: for rest, planks and holds, with quick +10 s and +1:00 buttons.
• Stopwatch: laps, splits, and your fastest and slowest lap.

CUES YOU CAN'T MISS
• 3-2-1 beeps before every change, plus distinct go and rest tones
• 5 sound styles: Classic, Boxing bell, Whistle, Soft and Digital
• Voice announcements in English, German, Spanish, French and Norwegian: "Round 3 of 8", "Halfway", "Last round"
• Haptic feedback and a full-screen colour flash on every interval change
• Lock your phone and keep going: Pulse still alerts you at every work/rest change
• Plays over your music instead of stopping it

TIMING YOU CAN TRUST
• Drift-free engine driven by a monotonic clock, accurate to the millisecond even after you switch apps
• Get-ready lead-in, pause, skip, go back, and hold-to-end so you never stop a workout by accident
• Keeps the screen awake while a timer runs
• Big, bold layouts in portrait and landscape, on iPhone and iPad
• Light and dark themes

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Save your favourite workouts and start them in one tap
• Classic benchmark workouts built in
• History with rounds, splits and personal records
• Weekly chart, training minutes and day streaks
• Share any workout as a link: friends open it straight in Pulse
• Back up and restore everything, or export your history as CSV

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Pulse works fully offline, and your workouts, history and settings never leave your device.

Made for WODs, HIIT, functional fitness, boxing and martial arts, running intervals, kettlebells, mobility, and anything else you put on the clock.
```
