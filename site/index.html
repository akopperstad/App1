Questions

Which workout formats does Pulse support?

AMRAP (including several AMRAPs with rest between), EMOM with any interval length (E2MOM, E90s…) and rotating movements, For Time with an optional cap and target rounds, Tabata, Intervals with rounds and sets, Death By, Custom sequences with timed and untimed steps, a simple Countdown and a Stopwatch with laps.

Will I hear the beeps when my phone is locked?

Yes. When you lock the phone or switch apps mid-workout, Pulse schedules an alert with sound at every work/rest change. Allow notifications when Pulse asks the first time you start a timer. More details.

Does it stop my music?

No. Beeps and voice play over your music. On iPhone you can switch this off in Settings if you prefer Pulse to take over the audio.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. Pulse works fully offline and has no sign-up. Your saved workouts and history live on your device; use Back up all data in Settings to keep a copy or move to a new phone.

Is it on iPad and Android tablets?

Yes. Pulse has layouts for tablets and landscape, so you can prop it up across the room.

Which languages are supported?

The app and its voice announcements are available in English, Norwegian, German, Spanish and French.
