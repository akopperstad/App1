Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

  • We collect no data. Pulse has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Everything stays on your device: settings, saved workouts and history.
  • The app makes no network requests and works fully offline.
  • No third parties receive anything from Pulse.

This policy explains how the Pulse workout timer app for iOS and Android ("Pulse", "the app") and this website handle information. Pulse is provided by TODO: developer legal name ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data or usage data. Pulse has no user accounts and contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software (SDKs). It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID) and does not track you across apps or websites.

2. Data stored on your device

To work, Pulse keeps the following in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your settings (sounds, voice, theme, language and similar preferences);
  • workouts you save;
  • your workout history (date, timer type, duration, rounds, laps and splits) and the personal records and weekly statistics calculated from it.

This data never leaves your device through Pulse. You can delete history inside the app (History › Clear all), and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud Backup on iPhone or Google's Android backup) may include app data in backups you have enabled. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account settings and their privacy policies; we have no access to them.

3. Backups, exports and shared workouts

Pulse can create a backup file or export your history as a CSV file, and can share a workout as a link. These only happen when you choose to, using your device's share or save sheet, and the file or link goes only where you send it. We never receive a copy.

A shared workout link contains only the workout settings (for example "Tabata, 20 s work, 10 s rest, 8 rounds" and any names you typed for the workout and its movements). It contains no personal data or history. The workout is stored in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers do not send to our website's server.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy Pulse asks
NotificationsRequested the first time you start a timer. While a workout runs with the screen locked or the app in the background, Pulse schedules local notifications on your device to cue each work/rest change. They are created on the device, not sent from a server, are cancelled when you return to the app, and are never used for marketing. You can decline or turn them off in system settings; the timer still works while the app is open.
Exact alarms (Android)Lets those workout cues fire on the exact second (a cue that arrives late is useless in a 20/10 Tabata).

Internet (Android)Declared by the app's built-in web view. Pulse does not use it to send or receive any data.

Voice announcements use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Pulse does not record audio and does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

5. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts and loads no external fonts. 6. Children

Pulse is a general-audience fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because Pulse collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

7. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete; all of your Pulse data is on your device and under your control. You can still contact us with any question.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of Pulse ever needed to collect any data, we would say so here and in the app stores' privacy labels before that version is released.
